### 5. Multi-Lingual & Multi-Currency Engine

1. **Languages:**
   - **EN (English):** Global international standard.
   - **RW (Kinyarwanda):** Native botanical nomenclature (*Ibihumyo by'igiti*, *Imegeri*, *Ikinyamugina*, Musanze terroir descriptions).
   - **FR (Français):** Regional and Francophone luxury clientele.
2. **Currencies:**
   - **RWF (Rwandan Francs):** Formatted (e.g., `38,000 RWF`).
   - **USD (US Dollars):** Formatted (e.g., `$28.00 USD`).
   - Conversion rate pegged at 1 USD ≈ 1,350 RWF with automatic rounding.
